Privacy notice

With consent, this application reads email content, attachments and metadata, calendar lists and events, and may manage calendar events when authorized. It uses this information for personal assistance, records and requested checks.

Credentials and working records are kept in access-controlled storage. Service providers may process data to operate the application, including AI processing of selected content. Data is not sold or used for advertising, and private content is not published on these pages.

Stored records and backups remain until the owner deletes them; service providers apply their own retention policies. Access can be revoked in account settings. Revocation stops future access but does not delete previously stored records. Contact information appears on the authorization screen.
